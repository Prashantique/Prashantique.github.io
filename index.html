About

I am Prashant Sharma, a penetration tester and security trainer based in Gwalior. I started in 2019 and have spent seven years on offensive security — more than 300 black box and grey box engagements across web, mobile, API, network and thick client, for clients in banking, financial services, energy, retail and enterprise technology.

I have led testing teams and owned delivery end to end: scoping, testing, reporting, executive debriefs and retest closure. On engagements I regularly find and exploit SQL injection, cross-site scripting, CSRF, remote code execution, privilege escalation, authentication bypass and business logic flaws, then sit with the development team while it gets fixed.

The other half of the job is teaching. I have trained more than 4,000 professionals and students, run corporate and college programmes, and mentor people through CEH, OSCP, CRTP and CREST.

That is also why I build tools. Roughly three quarters of an engagement is not testing — it is evidence, impact, remediation and report. Most online security tools tell you what is wrong and stop there. Mine give impact, reproduction steps, remediation and report-ready output, because that is the part that actually costs time. Where a browser genuinely cannot do something — enumerating TLS cipher suites, scanning ports — the tool says so rather than pretending.

Principles

  • 01Measure feasibility before promising a capability
  • 02Withhold a verdict rather than guess one
  • 03State plainly what a tool cannot determine
  • 04One tool should absorb a whole workflow
